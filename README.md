## How to…

### Find a wallpaper for your screen
1. Open [mijuru.com/wallpapers](https://mijuru.com/wallpapers).
2. Use the filters: device type (phone or desktop), resolution, colour, style.
3. Or open [Resolutions](https://mijuru.com/wallpapers/resolutions) and pick your exact screen size, or [Device brands](https://mijuru.com/wallpapers/device-brands) and pick your phone's brand.
4. Open any wallpaper to see it on a device frame before downloading.

### Download the right resolution
On a wallpaper page, choose one of the offered sizes. Badges such as **4K**, **2K** and **FHD** show the quality at a glance.

- **Desktop / laptop:** pick your monitor's resolution (for example 1920×1080, 2560×1440, 3840×2160). For an ultrawide monitor pick 21:9 or 32:9 if offered.
- **Phone:** pick a portrait (tall) version. If a wallpaper also exists in another orientation, the page links to it.
- If your exact size isn't there, take the next larger one with the same aspect ratio — your device will scale it down without losing quality.

### Get an avatar for a social network
1. Open [mijuru.com/avatars](https://mijuru.com/avatars) and pick a category or a network (Instagram, Discord, TikTok, Steam, WhatsApp, Facebook).
2. On the avatar page, check the round-crop preview — that's how it will look in a profile.
3. Download and upload it in your network's profile settings.

## Usage & licensing

- Wallpapers and avatars on mijuru.com are **free for personal use**: on your phone, computer, tablet, TV or social profile.
- **Not allowed:** reselling the images, or using them for commercial purposes.
- The Mijuru name and logo belong to Mijuru.

## Copyright & takedown requests

If you own the rights to an image on mijuru.com and want it removed or credited:

1. Open the image's page and use the **Report image** form, or
2. Email **m@mijuru.dev** with the page URL and proof of ownership.

Please don't file copyright requests as GitHub issues — they're public.

## FAQ

**Is Mijuru really free?**
Yes. Browsing and downloading are free, and you don't need an account.

**Do I need to sign up?**
No. Everything on the site works without an account.

**Which resolution should I choose?**
Your screen's native resolution. If it isn't listed, take the next larger size with the same aspect ratio. See [Download the right resolution](#download-the-right-resolution).

**Are there wallpapers for phones?**
Yes. Filter by device type, open [Device brands](https://mijuru.com/wallpapers/device-brands), or use the [Android app](#apps).

**Can I use a wallpaper on my YouTube channel, in an ad, or sell prints?**
No. The images are for personal use only.

**How often are new wallpapers added?**
Every day.

**How do I change the site language?**
Use the language menu on the site, or open the URL with a prefix such as `mijuru.com/uk/`.

**How do I switch between dark and light themes?**
Use the theme menu in the site header.

**I can't find a category or resolution I need.**
[Request it](https://github.com/orgs/mijuru-dev/discussions/categories/wallpaper-requests) — we read every request.

**Will there be accounts, favorites or collections?**
They're planned. Follow this repository for updates.
